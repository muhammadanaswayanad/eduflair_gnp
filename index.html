Who German Nursing Placement is for

German Nursing Placement is for people who have already passed a nursing degree or diploma. You are applying to work, not to join a study program.

This is you

  • GNM, BSc Nursing, Post-BSc, or an equivalent nursing diploma/degree.
  • You can complete (or already hold) German B2 across all four modules.
  • You want a job in Germany with a working salary, not a student stipend.

This is not Ausbildung

  • Ausbildung is vocational training with a monthly stipend while you study.
  • German Nursing Placement is for qualified nurses who will start work after language and processing.
  • If you finished 12th and want earn-while-you-learn, ask us about Ausbildung instead.

Questions nurses ask

Which nursing qualifications are eligible?+

GNM, BSc Nursing, Post-BSc, and comparable nursing diplomas or degrees. Tell us your exact certificate on the form so the counsellor can route you correctly.

What are the 4 German language modules?+

Reading, writing, listening, and speaking. You need to pass all four — a single-skill result is not enough for this pathway.

I don’t have German B2. Can I still apply?+

Yes. Choose “Need B2 modules” on the form. You can complete German B2 at Eduflair, then continue into German Nursing Placement processing.

How much will I earn in Germany?+

A nurse working in Germany can earn a starting salary of ₹3 Lakhs per month. You can earn up to ₹5 Lakhs- ₹7 Lakhs per Month with overtime and other benefits.
