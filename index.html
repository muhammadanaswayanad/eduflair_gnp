Who GNP is for

German Nursing Placement is for people who have already passed a nursing degree or diploma. You are applying to work, not to join a dual-study Ausbildung course.

This is you

  • GNM, BSc Nursing, Post-BSc, or an equivalent nursing diploma/degree
  • You can complete (or already hold) German B2 across all four modules
  • You want a job in Germany with a working salary, not a student stipend

This is not Ausbildung

  • Ausbildung is vocational training with a monthly stipend while you study
  • GNP is for qualified nurses who will start work after language + processing
  • If you finished 12th and want earn-while-you-learn, ask us about Ausbildung instead

Questions nurses ask

Is GNP the same as Ausbildung?+

No. Ausbildung is vocational training with a stipend. GNP is for nurses who have already completed a degree or diploma and will go to Germany to work after German B2 and placement processing.

Which nursing qualifications are eligible?+

GNM, BSc Nursing, Post-BSc, and comparable nursing diplomas or degrees. Tell us your exact certificate on the form so the counselor can route you correctly.

What are the four B2 modules?+

Reading, writing, listening, and speaking. You need to pass all four — a single-skill result is not enough for this pathway.

I don’t have German B2. Can I still apply?+

Yes. Choose “Need B2 modules” on the form. You can complete German B2 at Eduflair in Calicut, then continue into GNP placement processing.

How much will I earn in Germany?+

We present GNP salaries as ₹3.5 lakh to ₹4 lakh per month (INR equivalent of your pay in Germany). Your counselor will explain how region and recognition can affect the final figure.

When do I start working?+

After you hold B2 in all four modules and placement/visa processing is complete. If you already have B2, we skip language class and focus on the work file.
